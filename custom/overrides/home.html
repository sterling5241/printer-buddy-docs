Self-hosted · Klipper & Moonraker

Printer Buddy
One easy ecosystem.

Printer Buddy is a set of simple apps for your Klipper printers — AI failure detection, timelapses, notifications, a camera streamer and remote access. Pick the ones you want, install them with one script and keep them all up to date from one place. Run it on a printer or on a dedicated host. Klipper today, with support for more printers on the way.

Install it → See what it does

Already running it? Jump to Updates or Troubleshooting.

All your printers

Scan the network, select your printers, done. Status, progress, time left, temps and cameras for all of them, side by side.

AI failure detection

Spots spaghetti, warping, first-layer problems, stringing and more from the camera — then alerts you or pauses the print.

Full printer control

Move, heat, console, files, macros, bed mesh and a Save config button after calibrations — laid out the way you want.

Timelapses

A timelapse of every print, made by Printer Buddy and kept in one place.

Notifications

ntfy or Discord when prints start, pause, finish or fail — with a picture when the AI sees a problem.

From anywhere

Optional remote access and share links, so you can check a print from your phone away from home.

Up and running in three steps

  1. InstallClone the repo on a Pi, a server or a printer and run ./install.sh.
  2. Add printersScan the network and pick your Moonraker printers.
  3. Pick your appsTurn on the apps you want, and switch or update them any time from one place.
